Need to know

Frequently Asked Questions

A few quick answers before you submit a request.

Are the listed documents final?

No. The checklist is a starting guide. Final requirements depend on the official notification, portal and applicant details. Please confirm before applying.

How will I know the service charges?

Service charges will be discussed and confirmed on WhatsApp before proceeding. Any official government or portal fee is separate.

Does this website save my uploaded documents?

No. This is a static website and it does not store documents on a server. Depending on your device, selected files may be shared through the device share sheet; otherwise you may need to attach them manually in WhatsApp.

Does submitting a request guarantee approval?

No. Approval, eligibility and processing time are decided by the relevant department or authority.
